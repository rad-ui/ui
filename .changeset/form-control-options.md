Checkbox takes `indeterminate`, `uncheckedValue`, `form` and `inputRef`; Switch takes `uncheckedValue`, `form` and `inputRef`; RadioGroup takes `form` and `inputRef`. Form reset now also works with a `form` outside the tree. Slider takes `thumbCollisionBehavior` (`none`, `push`, `swap`), `format`/`locale`, `largeStep` and `form`; its thumbs take `getAriaLabel`, `getAriaValueText` and `inputRef`. NumberField takes `allowOutOfRange`, `snapOnStep`, `form` and `inputRef`.
