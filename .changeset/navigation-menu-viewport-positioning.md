`NavigationMenu.Viewport` can anchor itself to the open item's trigger: pass `side`, `align`, `sideOffset`, `alignOffset`, `anchor`, `collisionAvoidance`, `collisionPadding`, `collisionBoundary`, `positionMethod`, `sticky` or `disableAnchorTracking` and it is positioned with collision handling, with `data-side` / `data-align` for styling. Without these props it stays laid out by CSS as before.
