Add Autocomplete: a text input with suggestions. Unlike Combobox, the value is whatever the user types; typing opens a filtered list, ArrowDown and ArrowUp move through it while focus stays in the input, and Enter or a click fills in the suggestion. The Combobox primitive gains `inputValue`, `defaultInputValue` and `onInputValueChange` to control its search text.
