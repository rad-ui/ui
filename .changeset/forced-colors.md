The default theme now works in forced-colours mode (Windows High Contrast). Keyboard focus shows a system-colour outline (the theme's box-shadow rings are dropped in that mode), and parts that show state only with a fill (the Switch thumb and checked track, the Radio dot, the Slider range and thumb, Progress, the Tabs indicator, pressed toggles, the highlighted menu or list item) keep it in system colours.
